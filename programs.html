Education Tracks

Financial Wellness Programs across India

Six education-only, conflict-free learning tracks tailored for different stages of life, work, and community.

First-Salary & Early Career

Budgeting basics, emergency funds, understanding EPF, NPS, basic health insurance, and avoiding common financial traps for new professionals.

Employee Financial Wellness

Comprehensive workplace financial literacy: salary structuring, tax planning (Old vs New regime), debt management, mutual funds, and retirement planning.

Business Owners & Founders

Separating personal & business finances, cash flow management, business insurance, working capital strategies, and compliance awareness.

Retirees & Senior Citizens

Capital protection, regular income generation, SCSS, POMIS, annuity products, estate planning basics, and scam prevention for seniors.

Students & Young Adults

Foundational money habits, digital financial safety, education loan planning, compound interest power, and smart spending basics.

Community & Family Sessions

Household budgeting, joint financial goals, emergency preparedness, gold vs financial assets, and family financial security.
