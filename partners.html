Partner areas

Financial experts for every next step.

Each partner area supports one or more of WENA’s six financial wellness programs.

TAX & GST

Chartered accountants and tax practitioners

Income tax filing, tax-saving plans, GST registration and returns.

Supports: Students · Employees · Business Owners · Individuals
INVESTMENT PLANNING

SEBI-registered investment advisers

A personal investment plan based on your goals, risk and timeline.

Supports: Employees · Retirees · Individuals
INSURANCE

IRDAI-licensed insurance advisors

Reviewing cover, choosing term and health insurance, help with claims.

Supports: All six programs
WILLS & ESTATE DOCUMENTS

Advocates and documentation specialists

Wills, nominations, property papers and succession documents.

Supports: Retirees · Individuals & Families
BANKING & GOVERNMENT SCHEMES

Banking correspondents and scheme facilitators

Opening accounts and enrolling in savings, insurance and pension schemes.

Supports: Communities · Employees (frontline)
CREDIT & DEBT COUNSELLING

Credit counsellors

Understanding credit scores, planning debt repayment, escaping loan-app traps.

Supports: Employees (frontline) · Individuals
BUSINESS FINANCE

Accountants and MSME finance facilitators

Bookkeeping, cash-flow set-up, Udyam registration and MSME loan schemes.

Supports: Business Owners
FINANCIAL FRAUD SUPPORT

Cyber-fraud reporting and recovery specialists

Step-by-step help to report financial fraud and secure accounts quickly.

Supports: Retirees · Students · Individuals
