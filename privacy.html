Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how WENA (Wellness Education Network & Alliance) handles information when you visit wena.co.in.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you email us, join our WhatsApp community, request a session or contact an implementation partner through this site, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number and message. We use this only to respond to you and to deliver the programs you ask about.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the third-party services below may record standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you visit.

Advertisements shown on this site are placed by Google and are not endorsements by WENA. WENA provides financial education only and does not sell or recommend investment, insurance or loan products.

Third-party links

This site links to other websites and platforms (for example Amazon, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube). Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

How we protect and share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with implementation partners when you ask us to connect you, or where required by law.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have shared with us by writing to reachout@wena.co.in.
